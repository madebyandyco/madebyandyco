  ## Hi, I'm Andy
  Technical Lead and Creative Technologist with 15+ years in the digital industry. I build things that sit at the intersection of design and engineering — from polished front-end experiences to backend automation, logistics
  tooling, and the occasional trading bot.

  I work primarily in JavaScript across the full stack: Node.js for server-side work and automation, vanilla JS for lightweight modular frontends, and Google Apps Script for business process tooling that actually gets used. I
  like solving real problems with minimal unnecessary complexity.

  ### Currently building

  Operational and fintech tooling — real software used daily in production:

  - **Delta-neutral funding rate bot** — automated perpetual futures arbitrage on Bybit with live dashboard, position tracking and configurable risk management (CCXT, Node.js, Express)
  - **Logistics driver PWA** — offline-capable mobile delivery app with Google Sign-In, real-time Sheets sync, GPS-tagged proof-of-delivery photos and Leaflet map view
  - **Container status board** — live shipping operations dashboard with automatic status derivation and auto-refresh, backed by Google Sheets
  - **PDF watermark Chrome extension** — Manifest V3 extension for bulk PDF processing with progress tracking (pdf-lib)
  - **Google Sheets automation** — Apps Script runsheet processors and safety compliance tooling for logistics operations
