## Hi, I'm Andrew

I'm a software developer embedded in warehouse operations. As warehouse supervisor, I lead a small team, run interstate logistics, and manage stock across a 2,000+ SKU operation. I also build the tools that cut out errors and repetitive manual work.

After a decade as a developer, I moved into warehousing to see the whole system from the inside. Most of the friction I've found sits between platforms: stock counts that don't match, handoffs that drop, data keyed in by hand. I find those gaps and build the fixes.

### What I've recently built

- **Interstate delivery booking automation:** cut the process from 46 manual steps to four while monthly dispatches grew 61% since January 2026
- **Carrier tracking integrations:** live tracking for BEX, Mainfreight, DHL, MRL, FedEx and UPS, plus automated Twilio SMS delivery notifications
- **ERP and stock tooling:** Python/FastAPI and Playwright service exposing iDempiere orders, shipments and stock to Google Sheets, plus stock reconciliation between our WMS and our 3PL's system
- **Internal notification tool:** closed a recurring order-processing handoff failure and removed a daily source of follow-up emails
- **WHS automation:** automated recording of pre-starts, near-misses and toolbox meetings, with weekly reporting
- **Container status board:** live shipping dashboard for upcoming container deliveries and collections
- **Driver logistics PWA:** offline-capable delivery app with Google Sign-In, Sheets sync, GPS-tagged proof-of-delivery photos and map routing

### Side projects

- **PDF watermark Chrome extension:** bulk PDF processing (Manifest V3, pdf-lib)
- **Delta-neutral funding rate bot:** perpetual futures arbitrage with live dashboard and risk controls (CCXT, Node.js, Express)

### Tools I work with
