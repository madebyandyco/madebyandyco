## Hi, I'm Andy
Technical Lead and Creative Technologist with 15+ years in the digital industry. I build things that sit at the intersection of design and engineering — from polished front-end experiences to backend automation, logistics tooling, and the occasional trading bot.

I work primarily in JavaScript across the full stack: Node.js for automation and server-side work, React and Astro for interfaces, and Google Apps Script for business process tooling that actually gets used. I like solving real problems with minimal unnecessary complexity.
